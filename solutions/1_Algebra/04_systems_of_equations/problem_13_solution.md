### Solution 13. Does Every Equation Add New Information?

Given:

x1 + x2 = 10
x2 + x3 = 8
x1 - x3 = 2


Step 1. Express x1 using the first equation.

x1 = 10 - x2


Step 2. Express x3 using the second equation.

x3 = 8 - x2


Step 3. Check the third equation.

Substitute the expressions for x1 and x3:

x1 - x3 = 2

(10 - x2) - (8 - x2) = 2

10 - x2 - 8 + x2 = 2

2 = 2


Therefore, the third equation is automatically satisfied.

It does not provide any new information.


Step 4. Write the general solution.

Let x2 = t, where t is any real number.

From the first equation:

x1 = 10 - t

From the second equation:

x3 = 8 - t

Therefore:

x1 = 10 - t
x2 = t
x3 = 8 - t


Final Answer:

The general solution is:

(x1, x2, x3) = (10 - t, t, 8 - t)

where t is any real number.


Conclusion:

All three equations do not need to be used independently.

The third equation follows directly from the first two:

(x1 + x2) - (x2 + x3) = 10 - 8

x1 - x3 = 2

Therefore, the third equation is redundant.

The first two equations are sufficient to describe
the complete solution set.
